Privacy Policy

How the OPES School mobile application handles information.

Effective date: October 29, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how OPES School (the “App”), operated by OM PUSHPANJALI ENGLISH SCHOOL (“we”, “us”, or “our”), handles personal information through the OPES School mobile application and related school management services. Schools using the App administer their own students', families', and staff members' records.

1. Information we collect and process

Depending on the functions and permissions assigned by the school, the App processes:

  • Account details: user ID, name, school-assigned login details, user role, and contact information.
  • School records: student enrollment and profile information, class or section, attendance, examination results, and other academic information.
  • School administration records: staff information and related administrative records, where applicable.
  • Technical and security information: authentication and session information and limited technical records needed to provide and secure the service or diagnose problems.

We do not intentionally use the App to collect photos, device identifiers for advertising, push notification tokens, payment card details, third-party analytics data, or third-party crash-reporting data. The App may still process standard technical information needed to connect to and protect its servers. This statement applies to the current App functionality and will be updated if additional capabilities are introduced.

2. How we use information

We use the information to authenticate users; provide role-based access to student, attendance, and academic information; support school operations; maintain security; respond to authorized requests; and meet applicable legal and recordkeeping obligations. We do not sell student or staff personal information or use it for behavioral advertising.

3. Children’s and student information

The App handles school records that may include information about children. Accounts are created by authorized school administrators, not by public self-registration. Access to information is controlled according to the account holder's school-assigned role. Parents and guardians may contact the relevant school administration to request access to or correction of a child's information, subject to verification of their authority and applicable school policies.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information is made available only to the relevant users and authorized school personnel according to their permissions. The App communicates with its backend application and database infrastructure to deliver school management services. Technical providers supporting infrastructure or communications may process information only as needed to provide those services. We do not sell personal information or disclose school records to advertisers.

5. Storage and security

School information is maintained in the systems used to operate the App and is protected through account authentication and role-based access controls. We seek to limit access to authorized people and apply reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the information involved. No system can guarantee absolute security. Details about hosting location, encryption, backups, and any international data transfers may be requested from the privacy contact below.

6. Data retention, account management and deletion

Account management. OPES School accounts are created and managed exclusively by authorized school administrators. Users cannot sign themselves up for accounts in the App.

Requesting deletion or correction. A user, or an authorized parent or guardian acting for a student, may request account deactivation, eligible account deletion, correction, or deletion of personal data by contacting the school administration or by emailing pushpanjali10@gmail.com.

  1. Include your name, school, account role, and a description of the request. Do not send passwords or sensitive documents in an initial email.
  2. The school administration or authorized support personnel will verify the requester's identity and authority.
  3. After verification, the request will be reviewed, and information eligible for deletion will be deleted or anonymized where practicable.
  4. We or the school will communicate the outcome, including any categories of records that must be retained and the reason for retention.

Retention. Certain enrollment, attendance, examination, academic, and administrative records may need to be retained for legitimate educational, legal, or regulatory purposes. When deletion cannot be completed immediately, an administrator may deactivate access while reviewing the request. Records that must be retained will be protected and access-limited under applicable requirements. Retention periods depend on the school's recordkeeping obligations and applicable law.

There is no self-service account creation or account-deletion feature in the App. Deletion requests are handled through the school administration and the contact channel above.

7. Your choices and rights

You may request access to or correction of relevant personal information, or ask about deletion or restriction of processing, subject to applicable laws, educational recordkeeping obligations, and school policies. The school or support team may require identity and authorization verification before acting on a request.

8. Third-party links and services

If the App includes links to external websites, their privacy practices may differ from ours. Please review the relevant website's privacy policy before sharing information with it. The App does not currently integrate optional third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services as part of its stated functionality.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when App functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised whenever the policy changes.
